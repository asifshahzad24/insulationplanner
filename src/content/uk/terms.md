## Check before you buy or build

Before you start, check:

- **the Building Regulations.** Some insulation work counts as renovation under Approved Document L, and some projects need to be signed off by building control. The rules in Wales and Scotland differ from England's. If in doubt, ask your local building control body;
- **whether your home is suitable.** Cavity wall and solid wall insulation in particular need a survey, and some homes need specialist advice on moisture and condensation;
- **the product.** Thickness, λ and coverage vary between brands. Follow the manufacturer's data sheet and installation instructions; and
- **current prices.** The prices on this site are typical figures from a given date, including promotions, and will change. Installed costs are rough estimates, not quotes.

Where the site and a manufacturer's instructions, a professional's advice or the Building Regulations disagree, follow the instructions, the advice and the regulations.

## Safety

Insulation work can be hazardous. You're responsible for working safely and for deciding when to call in a professional. In particular:

- **Step only on joists** or on boards laid across them. A ceiling won't take your weight.
- **Electrics.** Don't disturb wiring, keep insulation clear of recessed downlights unless they're designed to be covered, and ask an electrician if you're unsure.
- **Ventilation.** Keep the eaves of a cold loft and the airbricks under a suspended floor clear.
- **Asbestos.** Pebbly loose fill in a loft may be vermiculite, which can contain asbestos. Don't disturb it, and follow the HSE's guidance.
- **Protective equipment.** Wear an FFP2 mask, goggles, gloves and long sleeves when handling mineral wool or cutting boards.
- **Installer-only work.** Cavity wall insulation and blown loft insulation should be installed by trained, registered installers.

## Where things stand today

At the time of writing, Insulation Planner carries **no affiliate links** and no advertising. Nobody pays us to mention a product, a brand or a store.

## How this may change

Running the site costs money, and we want to keep it free. To help cover those costs, some links on the site may become affiliate links in the future. These could include programs such as Amazon Associates or affiliate programs run by home improvement retailers.

If and when we add affiliate links:

- If you click one and buy something, we may earn a commission from the seller.
- The commission comes at **no extra cost to you**. You pay the same price you would otherwise.
- We'll label affiliate links clearly, close to the link itself, so you know before you click.
- We'll update this page and our [privacy policy](/privacy) before any affiliate links go live.

## What affiliate links will never change

- **The calculations.** Targets, depths, quantities and costs come from the published sources and formulas in our [methodology](/methodology). No retailer or manufacturer can change them, and a product's results don't depend on whether we earn anything from it.
- **Which products appear.** The calculator includes products because their manufacturers publish the coverage charts and data sheets we need, not because of any commercial relationship.
- **Our advice.** Safety notes, code reminders and tips are written to help you do the job right.

## Disclosure statement

In line with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's guidance on endorsements, we want you to know about any material connection between us and the products or stores we link to. Once the site carries affiliate links, this disclosure applies to them: **when you buy through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission, at no additional cost to you.**

Once we're enrolled in the Amazon Associates program, this statement will apply: **As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.**

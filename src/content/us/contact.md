## Email

**[info@loftinsulationcalc.com](mailto:info@loftinsulationcalc.com)**

We read every message and reply as soon as we can.

## What to write to us about

- **An error or an outdated number.** Tell us which page, what you entered and what looks wrong. If you have a source, such as a code table, a product data sheet or a current price, please include it. We check every report and fix confirmed errors.
- **A missing product or material.** Send the product name and a link to its data sheet or coverage chart.
- **Ideas.** New calculators, features or anything that would make the site more useful.
- **Press and partnerships.** Questions about the site, linking to it or working with us.

## What we can't help with

We can't give advice on a specific house, review a project or recommend a contractor. For that, talk to a home energy auditor or a local insulation contractor. Your utility may also offer a free or low-cost home energy audit.

## Your privacy

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply. See our [privacy policy](/privacy) for details, and our [about page](/about) for who runs the site.

## Check before you buy or build

Before you start, check:

- **the product label and coverage chart** on the insulation you actually buy, because coverage varies between brands and products;
- **your local building code**, since states and cities adopt and amend energy codes differently, and some projects need a permit and inspection; and
- **current prices** at your store, because the prices on this site are typical figures from a given date and will change.

Where the site and a product's label, the manufacturer's instructions or your local code disagree, follow the label, the instructions and the code.

## Safety

Insulation work can be hazardous. You're responsible for working safely and for deciding when to hire a professional. In particular:

- **Attics get very hot.** Work at a cool time of day, take breaks and drink water.
- **Walk only on joists** or boards laid across them. Ceiling drywall won't hold your weight.
- **Electrical wiring** can be damaged or dangerous. Don't disturb it, keep insulation away from non-IC-rated recessed lights, and have an electrician check old knob-and-tube wiring.
- **Chimneys, flues and vents** need clearance from insulation, as set by the product instructions and local code.
- **Vermiculite** insulation may contain asbestos. Don't disturb it; the EPA advises having it handled by a trained, accredited asbestos professional.
- **Protective gear:** wear an N95 mask, goggles, gloves and long sleeves when handling insulation.
- **Spray foam** should be installed by trained professionals.
